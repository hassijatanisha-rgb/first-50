The Piper voice model card identifies it as a single-speaker US English model trained from scratch on the LJSpeech dataset. The model collection is published under the MIT license. The LJSpeech recordings, transcripts, and annotations are public domain and have no use restrictions according to the dataset publisher.

- Model card: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/en/en_US/ljspeech/high/MODEL_CARD

Original scripts, visual layouts, diagrams, captions, and video edits are part of First 50. FFmpeg and Pillow were used locally to assemble the media. No Apple System Voice recording remains in this release.
